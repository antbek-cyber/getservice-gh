Privacy Policy - GetService GH

Last updated: Oct 5, 2026

GetService GH ("we") connects customers in Ghana with trusted artisans (plumbers, electricians, carpenters etc).

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, location (Kumasi, Accra etc) when you sign up as customer or worker
  • Profile photo and work skills for workers
  • Location to show nearby artisans
  • Job details and reviews

2. How We Use It

  • To match customers with nearby artisans
  • To show worker profiles, ratings and pricing (GHS/day)
  • To enable calls between customers and artisans
  • To process payments via Paystack

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We only share phone number and location between customer and artisan to complete a job. Payments are handled securely by Paystack.

4. Commission

Currently 0.00% promotional commission. Workers keep 100% of earnings.
