You must be at least {{ L.min_age }} years old to use {{ L.company }}.

2. Your account

  • Give true and current information, and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • One person, one account. Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Worker profiles are reviewed before they appear to customers. We may refuse or remove a profile if it does not meet these terms.

3. What we do and do not do

{{ L.company }} is a marketplace. We provide the tools for customers and workers to find each other, agree a price, and pay and review.

  • Workers are independent. They are not our employees, agents or partners.
  • The agreement to do a job is between the customer and the worker. We are not a party to it.
  • We do not guarantee the quality, safety, timing or legality of any work, or that a worker holds any licence or qualification they claim. Please check for yourself before you hire, especially for electrical, gas or structural work.

4. Bookings and prices

  • A booking starts as a request. The worker can accept or decline it.
  • The starting price is the worker's listed daily rate in Ghana cedis (GHS).
  • While a booking is pending or accepted, either side can propose a different price. A new price only applies if the other side accepts it. If you have already paid part of the price, you pay only the difference.
  • Once a booking is accepted, both sides should do what was agreed, including the date and place.

5. Payments

  • Customers pay through Paystack, using the payment methods Paystack offers. We never see or store your card or mobile money PIN.
  • You pay from your dashboard after the worker has accepted the booking. You will get a receipt and a payment reference.
  • We may charge a service fee. If we do, we will show it to you before you pay or before it is taken from a worker's earnings.
  • When a customer pays, the worker's earnings appear in their dashboard as "Pending payout" until we pay them out, and then as "Paid out". Payout timing can vary.
  • Please do not ask or agree to pay outside the app for a job booked through {{ L.company }}. Paying in the app protects both sides if there is a problem.

6. Cancellations, refunds and problems

  • A customer can cancel a booking request before it has been paid. A worker can decline a request before it has been paid.
  • Once money has been paid, the booking cannot be removed in the app. Contact us instead.
  • If the work was not done, or was not done as agreed, tell us within {{ L.refund_days }} days of the job date or of the worker marking it complete. We will look at what both sides say and may hold a payout, arrange a full or part refund, or take no action. Our decision is made fairly and in good faith, and your legal rights are not affected.
  • We encourage you to try to sort things out with the other person first.

7. Rules for workers

  • Describe your skills, experience, rate and location honestly. Only upload photos of your own work.
  • Do the work professionally and safely, turn up when you said you would, and tell the customer early if you cannot.
  • You are responsible for any licences, insurance, permits and taxes your work requires.
  • Use a customer's contact details only to deal with their job. Never share them or use them for anything else.

8. Rules for customers

  • Describe the job and the location accurately, and make sure the worker can safely get to the site.
  • Pay what was agreed, and treat workers with respect.
  • Jobs you post on the jobs board can be seen by anyone, so do not put private details such as your full address or ID numbers in the description.
